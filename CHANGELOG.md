# Changelog

Releases of Agent HUD Open. A version is a git tag `vX.Y.Z` or `vX.Y.Z-beta.N` on `main`; `CFBundleShortVersionString` in `scripts/build-app.sh` carries the three-part numeric version `X.Y.Z`. Each entry lists what changed for people using the application and, under **Host API**, what changed for applications that embed `AgentHUDCore` and `AgentHUDDesktop`. Dates are tag dates.

## Unreleased

- Simple and Dynamic docks never hide their logos. A reply or a request comes out of the logo of the agent that sent it and stands beside the dock, as it did with Separate agents on, while every other logo stays; before, with Separate agents off, the reminder took the dock's place and its card grew from the screen edge over the logos. An agent with no logo on that dock has its card come out of the dock's middle. Separate agents now only decides what hovering a logo opens: that agent's own panel, or the panel for every agent. Notch mode is unchanged.
- Host API: `ScreenHUD.dockMarks`, every mark on a dock whether or not it separates its agents. `LogoQueueConfig.resumes`, `SimpleMeterConfig.resumes`, `BotMark`'s `resumes` and `DockMemory.hide()` / `returns` are gone with the dock that hid under a card.

- A session its client archived or deleted leaves the session lists, the panel's, the Sessions page's and the phone's, while its tokens keep counting until the usage ledger expires them after 31 days. Archived are a Codex thread moved to `archived_sessions`, a Claude Code session archived in the Claude desktop app, an OpenCode session with `time_archived`, a Cursor composer its header marks archived, an OpenClaw session known only from its `.reset` or `.deleted` transcripts and a Qwen chat in `chats/archive`; each used to stay listed for its seven days. A log its client deleted no longer takes its tokens with it: they stay in the totals, unless a copy of the same session, a rollout Codex archived or a Claude Code log copied to another project's folder, counts in its place. OpenCode's plugin removes the turn records of a session OpenCode deletes, which kept it listed.
